# Code of conduct

We welcome respectful participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Discuss ideas and evidence, give constructive feedback, and respect privacy.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, publication of private information and unwanted sexual attention are not acceptable. Do not post customer records or another person's confidential information.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, limit participation, or remove access when necessary. Raise concerns privately with the repository maintainers. Reports will be handled with care and shared only as needed to address the situation. These expectations apply to this project's issues, pull requests, discussions and community spaces.
